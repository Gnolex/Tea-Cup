The Origin

Grown slowly,
because haste tastes
of nothing.

Our gardens sit on the flood plain of the Brahmaputra, where the soil is heavy with silt and the air stays thick from March until October. It is not a comfortable place to grow tea. That discomfort is the entire point — the leaf works harder, and the cup carries it.

Every bag is a second flush harvest, picked between late May and June, in the twelve days when the tips turn gold. Two leaves and a bud. Nothing beneath. The rest of the bush is left to the season.

We do not blend to a standard. We bottle a year, and each year arrives with its own weather inside it.
